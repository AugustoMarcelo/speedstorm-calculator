# Approved visual direction

Mode: Operate. Surface: single-route calculator.

Compact racing-crew dashboard: Barlow Condensed for headings and prominent numbers; Barlow for controls and supporting copy. Deep navy with elevated blue surfaces. Cyan marks selection and interaction; yellow identifies Stars and Racer Shards still needed. Restrained track geometry in the header; no remote imagery.

FIRST VIEWPORT: short title and calculator immediately visible. Desktop: form at left and result at right. Mobile: controls and result stack, with compact choices and a nearby summary. No promotional hero, Racer catalogue, or onboarding.

Primary interaction: selecting Stars, Star Fragments unlocked, and inventory Racer Shards updates the remaining shard count and Star-by-Star breakdown immediately. Use native radios with clickable labels, arrow-key operation, and visible focus. Changing the current Stars resets Star Fragment progress toward the next Star.
